Safety

Safety is a condition of the work, not an extra.

  • Activities are conducted safely.
  • The health of employees, visitors and the public is protected.
  • Environmental performance meets contemporary requirements.
  • Operations are run in a manner acceptable to local communities.
  • Employees, assets and information are protected from harm, damage or loss.

On site

  • Foam insulation is cast by a crew in full protective equipment.
  • Work at height on roof-level pipe racks.
  • Lifting and fixing of large duct sections.
