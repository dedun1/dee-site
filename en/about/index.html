Who we are

We work where the requirements are highest.

Daliah Al-Ezz Co. for Contracting was established in Jeddah in 2010 and is an electromechanical company: HVAC, plumbing, fire fighting, electrical and low current, with specialist works in insulation, aluminum cladding and chilled water joint treatment. Our crews have carried out scopes for main contractors on the Third Saudi Expansion of the Holy Mosque in Makkah and at King Abdulaziz International Airport in Jeddah.

An in-house technical team follows each project from inception to final handover. We join with other organizations in two ways: cooperating on large projects, or integrating services within one scope. Our workforce can be expanded when a project needs it through subcontractors and specialist labour.

Mission

To perform for our customers the highest level of construction services at fair and market-competitive prices. To ensure the longevity of our company through repeat and referral business, earned by customer satisfaction in timeliness, attention to detail and a service-minded attitude. To keep the highest levels of professionalism, integrity, honesty and fairness with our suppliers, subcontractors, associates and customers.

Vision

Every project we finish requires superior performance and workmanship. We hold our work to stringent quality and performance standards.

Core values

Five values that run through the work.

Integrity

We trust and can be trusted. Integrity guides how we deal with people. We stand by what we say and take accountability for every decision.

Quality

We do not aim to meet a client's expectations. We aim to exceed them.

Human capital

Our most valuable asset is our team. We keep a learning culture and develop our people.

Innovation

We will always find a way. We encourage our people to improve how every part of the work is done.

Sustainability

We respect our environment and community, and conserve resources so we can keep serving that community for generations.

Safety

Safety is a condition of the work, not an extra.

  • Activities are conducted safely.
  • The health of employees, visitors and the public is protected.
  • Environmental performance meets contemporary requirements.
  • Operations are run in a manner acceptable to local communities.
  • Employees, assets and information are protected from harm, damage or loss.
