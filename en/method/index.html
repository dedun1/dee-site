Work is done in sequence, and recorded at every step.

This is how we take on any scope, and how we treated the chilled water joints in the utility culverts of the Holy Mosque.

From study to handover

Six stages on every project.

  1. 01Study and pricing

    Tender documents and drawings reviewed, quantities taken off, cost estimated.

  2. 02Engineering and approvals

    Design review and value engineering, shop drawings, material submittals for approval.

  3. 03Procurement and planning

    Equipment and materials procured, programme set.

  4. 04Execution

    Installation by our own crews under technical supervision at every stage.

  5. 05Inspection and quality

    Inspection and quality control with the main contractor and the consultant.

  6. 06Testing and handover

    Testing and commissioning, then operator training and handover.

Worked example

Chilled water joint treatment.

Utility culverts and piazza, Third Saudi Expansion of the Holy Mosque, Makkah. Photos are from site and carry the crew's own labels.

  1. Step 01

    Surface preparation

    Rust and old coating are removed from the joint by power tool until clean steel is exposed around the full circumference.

  2. Step 02

    Roughness check

    The surface profile is measured with a gauge and recorded against the joint number before any coating is applied.

  3. Step 03

    Primer coat and thickness check

    The primer coat goes on, then its film thickness is measured and logged.

  4. Step 04

    Final protective coat

    The approved protective paint is applied by hand to the specified finish.

  5. Step 05

    Mold preparation

    A sheet-metal mold is strapped around the joint to form the insulation to the pipe diameter.

  6. Step 06

    Foam insulation casting

    The two-part foam is mixed on the scaffold and cast into the mold by a crew in full protective equipment.

  7. Step 07

    Finished insulation and handover

    The mold is removed, the joint is inspected and the work is handed over to the consultant. Aluminum cladding follows where specified.
