Insulation and aluminum cladding

Cladding of ducts, pipes and equipment, and treatment of chilled water joints.

Overview

Large chilled water networks only perform if every pipe, duct, valve and joint is protected. We prepare and coat pipe joints, cast foam insulation around them and finish ducts, pipes and equipment in aluminum cladding. This work has been carried out across the utility areas of the Third Saudi Expansion of the Holy Mosque.

Scope

  • Aluminum cladding of ducts
  • Aluminum cladding of pipes, valves and equipment
  • Surface preparation and rust removal at pipe joints
  • Protective coating of chilled water joints
  • Foam insulation casting at pipe joints
  • Pre-insulated chilled water piping

Common questions

Before you send the scope.

How do you treat chilled water pipe joints?

In a fixed sequence: surface preparation, roughness check, primer and final coat with thickness checks, mold preparation, foam insulation, then handover to the consultant. The full sequence is shown in photos on the How we work page.

Do you work as a subcontractor under a main contractor?

Yes. Most of our work is carried out as a subcontractor, including packages on the Third Saudi Expansion of the Holy Mosque in Makkah and at King Abdulaziz International Airport in Jeddah.

Where do you work?

Our office is in Jeddah and the projects on this site are in Makkah and Jeddah. For other cities, send us the location and we will tell you if we can cover it.

What do you need from us to price a job?

Drawings or a bill of quantities if you have them, the project location and the planned start date. A short description is enough to begin, and we come back with our questions.

Can we get your company documents for pre-qualification?

Yes. Choose "Request the pre-qualification pack" in the enquiry form and we send the company records.
