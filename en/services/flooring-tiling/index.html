Common questions

Before you send the scope.

What kinds of buildings do you finish?

Residential, commercial and service buildings. The projects on this site include a multi-storey residential building in Jeddah, a gym fit-out and villas in Makkah.

Do you work as a subcontractor under a main contractor?

Yes. Most of our work is carried out as a subcontractor, including packages on the Third Saudi Expansion of the Holy Mosque in Makkah and at King Abdulaziz International Airport in Jeddah.

Where do you work?

Our office is in Jeddah and the projects on this site are in Makkah and Jeddah. For other cities, send us the location and we will tell you if we can cover it.

What do you need from us to price a job?

Drawings or a bill of quantities if you have them, the project location and the planned start date. A short description is enough to begin, and we come back with our questions.

Can we get your company documents for pre-qualification?

Yes. Choose "Request the pre-qualification pack" in the enquiry form and we send the company records.
